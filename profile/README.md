## About Us

MiidayStudio is a digital studio focused on building websites, web applications, and digital products.

We combine design, development, and technology to create useful, modern, and reliable solutions.

**Established:** 2026  
**Location:** Mumbai, India  
**Working:** Worldwide (Remote)

## What We Do

- **Web Development** – Modern and responsive websites.
- **UI/UX Design** – Clean and user-friendly designs.
- **Full-Stack Development** – Complete web applications.
- **Animations & Interactions** – Smooth and engaging experiences.
- **Cloud & Backend** – Reliable systems and APIs.
- **AI & Automation** – Smart tools and workflows.
